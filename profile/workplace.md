## How decisions are made here

- Answer first. The recommendation is on slide one or two, not at the end.
- One-pagers and tight decks. Appendix for detail.
- "So what" is asked constantly. Every finding needs a decision attached.
- Well-managed is a real standard: controls, monitoring and documentation are
  expected before launch, and Risk partners are consulted early, not at the end.
- Test-and-learn culture: proposals are expected to come with a test design or
  a reason one is not needed.
- Data-driven to a fault: a number on a slide will be challenged on its
  provenance.
